# Scoring Principles

## Do Not Build Yet
WF3 scoring is blocked until intake data exists.

## Future Scoring Inputs
- keyword demand
- keyword competition
- trend direction
- estimated product sales/revenue
- listing growth
- live Etsy competition context
- manual originality/risk review
- margin assumptions

## Score Requirements
Every score must include source evidence, confidence, risks, and recommended next action.

## Avoid Fake Precision
Do not pretend estimates are exact.
